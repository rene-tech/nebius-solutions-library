DRAFT

**Complaint/History**
- Diarrhea for the last three days, described as loose and watery stools, occurring 6–7 times per day.
- Associated lower abdominal pain, localized to the left side, described as cramp-like, intermittent, and non-radiating.
- Vomiting at the onset of symptoms, now resolved; vomit was food-colored with no blood.
- Feverish sensation at symptom onset (3–4 days ago), but no measured temperature; no current fever.
- Weakness, shakiness, and loss of appetite.
- Able to tolerate fluids, soups, and smoothies.
- Symptoms began after eating at a Chinese restaurant four days ago.
- One child had vomiting but no diarrhea; no other household members affected.
- Symptoms impacting daily activities, including need to stay close to the toilet; patient has continued going to work (accountant).

**Relevant Background**
- Asthma, well-controlled on inhaler.
- No other chronic bowel or gastrointestinal conditions reported.

**Medications**
- Inhaler for asthma.
- No other medications.

**Allergies**
- Not documented.

**Findings**
- Left lower abdominal pain, cramp-like, intermittent.
- No blood in stool or vomit.
- No current fever, but subjective fever at onset.
- No sweating reported.
- No travel history, smoking, or alcohol use.

**Clinician's Assessment**
- Likely acute gastroenteritis, possibly post-food ingestion (Chinese takeaway).
- Viral etiology suspected; bacterial cause not ruled out.

**Plan/Follow-up**
- Conservative management: oral rehydration (e.g., Dioralyte), continued fluid intake.
- Paracetamol 2 tablets up to 4 times daily for fever and discomfort in the first few days.
- Advised to take 2–3 days off work and rest.
- Return for review if symptoms persist beyond 3–4 days; stool sample may be required if no improvement.
- No antibiotics prescribed at this stage.
- Treatment plan discussed and understood; no questions from patient.
