DRAFT

**Complaint/History**
- Diarrhea for the last three days, described as loose and watery stool, occurring 6–7 times per day.
- Associated lower abdominal pain, localized to the left side, described as cramp-like, coming and going.
- Initial vomiting at symptom onset, now resolved; vomit was food-colored with no blood.
- Subjective fever at symptom onset (3–4 days ago), no measured temperature; no current fever.
- Feels weak and shaky; no night sweats.
- Loss of appetite; able to tolerate fluids and light foods (soups, smoothies).
- Symptoms began after eating at a Chinese restaurant four days ago; no other family members with identical symptoms (one child had vomiting only).
- Symptoms affecting daily life, requiring proximity to toilet; patient has continued going to work (accountant).

**Relevant Background**
- History of asthma, well-controlled with inhaler.
- No other known bowel or gastrointestinal conditions.
- Lives with wife and two children.

**Medications**
- Inhaler for asthma.
- No other medications documented.

**Allergies**
- Not documented.

**Findings**
- No blood in stool.
- No blood in vomit.
- No current vomiting.
- Pain is localized to left lower abdomen, cramp-like, intermittent, not radiating.
- No measured fever; patient felt hot at onset.
- No alcohol use.
- Non-smoker.

**Clinician's Assessment**
- Likely gastroenteritis, possibly post-infectious, potentially linked to recent takeaway meal.
- Viral etiology suspected; bacterial cause not ruled out.

**Plan/Follow-up**
- Conservative management advised: hydration, use of oral rehydration solutions (e.g., Dioralyte).
- Paracetamol as needed: up to two tablets four times daily for fever and weakness.
- Advised to take 2–3 days off work to rest.
- Review in 3–4 days if symptoms persist; possible stool sample testing if no improvement.
- No antibiotics prescribed at this time.
- Patient agrees with plan and has no further questions.
