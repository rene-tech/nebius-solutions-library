DRAFT

**Complaint/History**
- Diarrhea for the last three days, described as loose and watery stools.
- Bowel frequency: 6–7 times per day.
- Lower abdominal pain, localized to the left side, described as cramp-like, intermittent.
- Vomiting at symptom onset, now resolved; vomit was food-colored, no blood.
- Feeling weak and shaky; no measured fever, but felt hot at symptom onset (~3–4 days ago).
- Loss of appetite; able to tolerate fluids and light foods (soups, smoothies).
- Symptoms began after eating takeaway food from a Chinese restaurant four days ago.
- No other household members have diarrhea; one child had vomiting but not diarrhea.
- Symptoms affecting daily activities, requiring proximity to toilet; patient has continued working as an accountant.

**Relevant Background**
- Asthma, well-controlled with inhaler.
- No other known gastrointestinal conditions.
- No other medications besides inhaler.
- Non-smoker, does not consume alcohol.

**Findings**
- No blood in stool or vomit.
- Pain is localized to left lower abdomen, crampy, intermittent; does not radiate to back.
- No current fever, sweating, or other systemic symptoms.
- Able to maintain hydration with fluids.

**Clinician's Assessment**
- Likely acute gastroenteritis, possibly foodborne (viral or bacterial).
- No signs of severe infection or dehydration requiring immediate intervention.

**Plan/Follow-up**
- Conservative management: oral rehydration (e.g., Dioralyte).
- Paracetamol 2 tablets up to four times daily for fever and weakness in first few days.
- Advised to take 2–3 days off work to rest.
- Return for follow-up if symptoms persist beyond 3–4 days.
- Consider stool sample testing if symptoms continue.
