# Current model startup measurements — 7 September 2026

Measured on the deployed H100 cluster in project `project-e00rene`,
`eu-north1`. These are **fresh-process starts with existing cached images and
weights**, not disk-cold downloads or GPU snapshot restores. No model image,
weights, precision, inference work size, resource limit, quota or driver was
changed. The retained production Qwen service stayed available.

## Initialization measurements

Three observations per profile; seconds from **Kubernetes container start**
to the recorded native initialization or serving-application readiness marker.
Ranges are minimum–maximum, not confidence intervals or tail percentiles.

**Scientific initialization is not fully warmed inference.** Lazy compilation
can follow these markers. Mosaic/BindCraft's JAX effects barrier does not prove
completion of pure-array device work; those are native initialization markers,
not synchronized GPU-ready promises. AlphaFold3's median container-to-first
validated complete output was **33.43 seconds**; its 8.09-second host loader must
not be marketed as an 8-second GPU cold start. Multi-stage scientific workflows
also initialize later models/stages separately.

Kubernetes container timestamps have one-second granularity; extra decimal
digits preserve the source calculations, not sub-millisecond clock accuracy.
Shared nodes and immutable weight caches were retained. Fixed small acceptance
fixtures make measurements repeatable; they do not establish latency for arbitrary
protein sizes, larger complexes, batches, seeds or MSA searches.

## Infrastructure and first-result overhead

For existing cached nodes, Qwen's median Pod-to-Kubernetes-ready was **110 s**
and Cosmos's was **60 s**. Their first validated text/video responses arrived
**114.46 s / 64.41 s** after Pod creation. Those isolated timings include
readiness observation and connection setup, not a public activation request.

An additional Cosmos start provisioned a **new preemptible H100 node** using
the original autoscaler envelope. It took **444 s** from Pod creation to
Kubernetes-ready and **449.55 s** to the first validated video. Scheduling/node
wait accounted for 154 s, the 9.187 GB image pull for 142.693 s, and application
initialization for 66.099 s. The shared weight PVC was still cached. This is
one separate sample, excluded from the three cached-image measurements.

Scientific Pod-to-initialized and complete validated-output times, phase
boundaries, exact fixtures and artifact hashes are in the detailed reports.
Do not substitute full design or batch duration for startup. In particular,
BoltzGen's complete 20-candidate design stage took median **288.32 s** after
container start, distinct from its **36.05 s** initialization.

## Evidence and completion

All 36 principal trials completed with their full configured scientific,
text or video output validated, plus one separate successful new-node Cosmos
trial. BindCraft's slowest complete native filter search is retained rather
than mistaken for startup or omitted. Setup/observer failures and unmeasured
control runs are disclosed in the lane reports. All task-owned probe resources
and parent watch/log-follow processes were stopped or removed; production
services and normal autoscaling remain running. The completed lane reports
and private capture hashes preserve measurements independently of ephemeral Pods.
