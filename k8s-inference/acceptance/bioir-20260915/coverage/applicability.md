Public BIR source inspected: `401c6fcc4a43925bcf1342b6c0979b060130b396` on 2026-09-15. None of these eight product models appears as a supported model/module in the [BIR support matrix](https://docs.nvidia.com/bionemo/inference-runtime/references/support-matrix/). That does not preclude carefully tested reuse of an individual tensor operation. ## Related scientific products: explicit indirect assessment

RFdiffusion's retained execution logs confirm the checkpoint uses `d_hidden=32` and `d_pair=128`, matching BIR OPM's fixed `(C,D,C_z)=(32,32,128)` dimension gate. The [CuTe dispatch](https://github.com/NVIDIA-BioNeMo/BioNeMo-Inference-Runtime/blob/401c6fcc4a43925bcf1342b6c0979b060130b396/bionemo_ir/_torch/custom_ops/outer_product_mean/cutedsl.py) accepts only FP16/BF16, not FP32. The actual operation dtype has not been instrumented here; changing precision merely to enter this kernel would require a new numerical-quality comparison. The configuration also explicitly notes that its SM89 constrained tile remains unvalidated. Thus this is a bounded feasibility lead, not an executed RFdiffusion acceleration result.

These are distinct products, not additional BioNeMo-branded coverage claims. Their live scientific execution configurations are retained in the inventory. No benchmark of an unrelated substituted model counts for them.

## Snapshot and serving implications

ProteinMPNN, DiffDock, GenMol, MolMIM and Evo2 currently retain runtime/model state. Their measured first-shape and repeated-call differences already contain residency/cache effects. They cannot be credited to BIR. RFdiffusion and Complexa launch process-oriented scientific stages; retaining an upstream worker may improve loading cost independently of a kernel change.

CUDA graphs in BIR capture eligible diffusion computations. They do not establish process snapshot capture or restore. This lane does not claim a snapshot experiment. Snapshot compatibility, fresh-pod restore, a new input shape, recovery and GPU-memory cleanup are owned by the dedicated snapshot lane. No snapshot conclusion for one runtime is transferred to these eight models.
