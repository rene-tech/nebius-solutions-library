# Four-engine molecular-dynamics acceptance results

This is the tracked copy of `RESULTS.md` from the complete delivered artifact
bundle. File links below are relative to that bundle, whose server path is
`/home/tux/fs2-alanine-comparison-20260923/delivery-02`; raw trajectories and
rendered videos are intentionally not duplicated into the source repository.

All four requested native engines completed minimization, 100 ps NVT,
100 ps NPT and 1 ns NPT production. Two unchanged-input hosted cohorts per
engine passed duration, frame, finite-coordinate and artifact checks. The
comparison below uses the first final-qualified cohort, not historical variants.

One AmberTools master defines ACE–ALA–NME, Amber ff14SB, TIP3P and the same
6,598 atom ordering. Minimum original peptide clearance exceeds 1.0 nm on
every axis. Hydrogen bonds and water geometry are constrained; production uses
2 fs steps, a 1 ps coordinate interval and 10 Å cutoffs. All four use explicit
64³/order-4 PME or PPPM; GROMACS autotuning is disabled. Native thermostat,
barostat, constraint and numerical implementations are disclosed in REPRODUCE.md.

## What the validation does and does not establish

- The conversion audit explicitly checks mapped atom types, masses, charges,
  bonded potential curves, Lennard-Jones coefficients and combining rules,
  exclusions, and Amber SCEE=1.2/SCNB=2.0. Corrupted-parameter negative controls
  fail. Execution success is not used as topology-equivalence evidence.
- Canonical-coordinate bonded energies agree to about 0.0001 kcal/mol. Total
  potential energies span 1.126109 kcal/mol because native electrostatic
  constants, numerical implementations and dispersion-tail conventions differ.
  These differences are measured and retained, not corrected by changing charges.
  Full-force-array comparison was obtained for GROMACS/LAMMPS only; four-engine
  force parity is **not** claimed.
- Amber prints a midpoint kinetic-temperature estimator. Independently measured
  current-velocity temperature is 300.181 K for these exact H100 output bytes.
  The table retains the actual printed 298.328 K rather than replacing it.
- Pressure sample standard deviations are about 263–270 bar. A 1 ns mean is not
  precise equilibrium pressure. Native pressure definitions also differ; NAMD's
  group-pressure statistic is identified explicitly. These correlated samples
  do not establish converged ensembles or free energies.
- The LAMMPS native continuation reprojects SHAKE coordinates at process setup.
  An exact-source/hash-bound independent geometric solve explains the small
  boundary displacement. Both native records and the original strict failure
  remain retained. This is native checkpoint continuation, **not GPU snapshotting**.
